Why we built it

The rules exist. Most travelers never use them.

Boston to Los Angeles, connecting in Denver. The Denver flight slipped. Because the delay was United’s, and with status, the traveler could move to the Boston nonstop instead. On Delta or American that same move would only open up after a disruption.

That option depends on the airline, fare, status, the cause of the delay, and which seats are open that morning. Rerouted puts all of it together the moment something changes, and tells you the move in plain words.

How it works

From confirmation email to a better flight

Four steps, and only the first one is yours.

  1. Forward your confirmation

    Paste or forward the email from United, Delta, American, Southwest or the site you booked on. Rerouted reads your flights, fare, status and who sold the ticket, and flags risks like separate tickets.

  2. Rerouted watches

    Live flight status, the plane coming in, and weather at your connection. You hear about trouble the night before, while it’s still easy to fix.

  3. You get the move

    The flight to switch to, why you’re owed it, the fastest line to use, and a script to read or show the agent.

  4. You get your money back

    When your card’s coverage kicks in, Rerouted builds the claim: deadlines on real dates, a documents checklist, and details ready to paste.

Under the hood

Built on the actual rules, and live data

Rerouted only shows what it can actually know: live flight data, published rules, and what you tell it. It never guesses at seat counts, standby lists, wait times or where your bag is.

The rules it knows

  • Same-day changes and standby on four airlinesBy fare and status, including Delta and American’s same-routing rule and Southwest’s fare-based rules
  • Each airline’s disruption commitmentsRebooking, meals, hotels, partner airlines and Southwest’s travel credit
  • DOT refund rulesCancellations and 3+ hour domestic delays
  • DOT delay-cause categoriesThe Section 511(b) rule in effect since October 19, 2026
  • DOT bag fee refundsBags more than 12 hours late on domestic flights
  • Card trip and baggage delay termsThresholds, limits, deadlines and where to file

The data it watches

  • Live flight status and alertsDelays, cancellations, diversions and gate changes
  • The inbound aircraftWhere your plane is coming from, and how late
  • Gates and arrival timesWhere you land, where your next flight leaves, and the clocks for refunds and coverage
  • Airline schedulesEvery alternative flight to your destination
  • Seats for saleWhether a better flight can be booked for your party, so you know to ask for a confirmed seat or stand by
  • Airport disruptions and weatherGround stops, delay programs and forecasts at your hubs
  • Airline travel waiversFree changes issued before storms

What you tell it

  • Why the flight is lateNo public feed has the cause. You confirm what United said, and the advice adjusts.
  • Your bag report numberBag tracking isn’t public. The report number links your claim.
  • Whether you cleared standby or got rebookedAirlines don’t share this publicly. One tap keeps Rerouted tracking the right flight.
  • Which cards you carryRerouted checks each card’s coverage. No bank login needed.
